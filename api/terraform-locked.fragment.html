Problem

Terraform users with read-only access could review configurations, but limiting edit permissions also risked removing the context they needed to understand what was configured. My challenge was designing a read-only experience that provided enough information to investigate and understand configurations without giving users unnecessary control.

Auditing the Existing Experience

I reviewed 41 configuration routes across Terraform to identify recurring read-only states and determine where a consistent interaction pattern could scale across the product.

The goal is for the full audit to communicate scale at first glance, while giving interested recruiters the option to explore the actual work in more detail.
