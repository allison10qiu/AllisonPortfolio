Making read-only access clearer in Terraform

During my Product Design internship at IBM, I worked on Terraform’s read-only experience for users who could review configurations but didn’t have permission to change them.

What seemed like a permissions change affected much more than the Edit button. Across the product, removing editing controls could also remove context people needed to understand what was configured.

The problem

Read-only turned out to be more than removing the edit button.

Terraform’s role-based access control decides who can view a setting and who can change it. Making a setting read-only raised a harder question: what should stay visible once the controls are gone?

Edit Read-only
Value Current configurationSelected value Context i depends on the value above Editing SaveCancel Removed in read-only
EditableCurrent value + Context + Editing controls Read-onlyCurrent value + Necessary context − Editing controls

Removing editing controls was straightforward. Deciding what information still helped someone understand the configuration was the harder part.
