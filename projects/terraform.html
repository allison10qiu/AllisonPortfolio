Internship Overview

I designed for the space between having access and having control.

Enterprise products aren’t always as simple as whether someone can or can’t access something. Different users need different levels of visibility and control depending on their responsibilities. My work focused on understanding those differences and designing experiences that make what users can see, what they can change, and why easier to understand.

Scaled a read-only pattern across 41 routes

Designed a reusable permission experience across 41 Terraform settings and configuration routes, creating a consistent way for users to understand what they could view versus modify.

Validated designs with 10+ users

Developed a multi-segment research plan and tested concepts with 10+ recruited participants, internal and external, to understand how users review, investigate, and navigate complex enterprise systems.

Designed across 3 distinct enterprise workflows

Translated complex authorization behavior into reusable UX patterns informed by the needs of auditors, support engineers, and platform engineers, balancing usability with security and technical constraints.
