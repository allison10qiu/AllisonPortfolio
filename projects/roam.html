Turning scattered travel inspiration into shared experiences

ROAM is a social travel app concept that brings community-driven discovery, personalized place ratings, digital postcards, and curated travel guides together, so the places people save, the trips they take, and the memories they make can live in one place.

Role
Lead Designer
Made for
SVSD Design-a-Thon 2026
Timeline
October 2026
Focus
  • Social discovery
  • Storytelling
Problem
Discovering a place, remembering it, and sharing it happen in different apps, so very little of what people find or remember stays connected.

Opportunity

The tools we use to discover places rarely connect with how we remember them

We save places on TikTok, collect travel inspiration on Pinterest, and take hundreds of photos that end up buried in our camera rolls. Discovering a place, remembering it, and sharing it happen in different apps, so very little of what we find or remember stays connected.

TikTok · saved placesPinterest · inspirationCamera roll · photos
ROAM, in one place

Discovering, remembering and sharing a place, together.

What we designed

Discovery, memories, and guides in one app

  1. 01

    Discover through people, not just places

    Destinations come with community perspectives and factor-based ratings that reflect different travel priorities.

  2. 02

    Turn memories into postcards

    Travel moments become visual keepsakes that are easy to personalize, revisit, and share.

  3. 03

    Curate stories into travel guides

    Favorite places, recommendations, and memories come together in personalized travel guides.

This is a short preview. A full case study is coming soon.
