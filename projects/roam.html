What if the places we save, the trips we take, and the memories we make could all live in one place?

ROAM is a social travel app concept that combines community-driven discovery, personalized place ratings, digital postcards, and curated travel guides to make exploring and documenting the world more connected and personal.

Watch Demo↗

The Opportunity

We save places on TikTok, collect travel inspiration on Pinterest, and take hundreds of photos that end up buried in our camera rolls. But the tools we use to discover places rarely connect with how we remember and share them.

What we designed

01

Discover through people, not just places

Explore destinations through community perspectives and factor-based ratings that reflect different travel priorities.

02

Turn memories into postcards

Transform travel moments into visual keepsakes that are easy to personalize, revisit, and share.

03

Curate stories into travel guides

Bring favorite places, recommendations, and memories together in personalized travel guides.
