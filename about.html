Hey, I’m Allison

I got into design through two things: a love for psychology and an embarrassing amount of time spent perfecting my Instagram photos. Design turned out to be the perfect mix of both: understanding how people think, and caring a little too much about how things look.

I like figuring out why something feels harder than it should. My favorite part of a project is the beginning, when users, the team and the tech don’t quite line up yet. To get better at that last piece, I’ve been getting out of Figma more, learning to build things myself and breaking a lot of stuff along the way.

When I’m not figma-maxxing, I’m probably crocheting a surprise gift, baking a new recipe, or researching the next concert to go to.
