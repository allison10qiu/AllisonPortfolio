## Featured Projects

### 1. Terraform — Read-only Governance (IBM)
**Role**: Product Design Intern (Summer 2026)
**Status**: Featured Project

**Problem**: Users with read-only roles in Terraform couldn't access critical settings to understand organization structure and compliance requirements. This created friction and forced workarounds.

**Solution**: Designed a role-based access experience that lets read-only users view governance and compliance information without the ability to modify settings, improving visibility while maintaining security.

**Key Focus Areas**:
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Governance and compliance visibility
- Security without sacrificing transparency
- Enterprise user workflows

**View Full Case Study**: https://www.allisonqiu.com/projects/terraform
